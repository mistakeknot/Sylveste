## What the bead claims (and what's load-bearing)

OptiQ = sensitivity-aware mixed-precision quantization (per-layer bit-widths via
KL-divergence calibration). The bead's case for evaluating it on Qwen3.6-27B:

1. "Same size, higher quality" than uniform 4bit (16.5 GB vs ~14 GB).
2. OptiQ paper claims **+40–62% decode speedup at 64k context** (M3 Max).
3. Scope: add a `27b-3.6-optiq` config, an alias, A/B against plain 27b-3.6.
4. Explicitly depends on Sylveste-xzw to first establish the 27b-3.6 baseline.

The bead is honest about its own risks: card released the day it was filed, zero
downloads, no community validation, and the speedup claim is from **Qwen3.5 small
models (2B/4B/9B), not Qwen3.6-27B** — "unclear if it transfers."

## Why OptiQ cannot plausibly flip the verdict

The 27B's two deficits decompose cleanly, and OptiQ addresses neither:

1. **Speed deficit (4×): architectural, not precision.** 27B dense activates ~27B
   params/token; 35B-A3B activates ~3B. On a bandwidth-bound M5, decode speed tracks
   active-param bytes moved. OptiQ shaves bytes-per-param within a precision budget;
   it does not reduce the active-param *count*. Even a generous OptiQ decode speedup
   (the bead's own +40–62% — and that figure is for 2B–9B models at 64k context, not
   a 27B dense model at ~2K-token coding prompts) would close at most part of a 4×
   gap, leaving 27B-OptiQ still slower than the MoE and far slower than its quality is
   worth.
2. **Quality deficit (−1.7 points vs 35B-A3B): unrelated to quant scheme.** OptiQ's
   pitch is "same size, *higher quality than uniform 4bit*" — i.e., it recovers
   quantization loss vs plain 4bit of the *same model*. It does not make a 27B model
   smarter than a different, better-suited 35B MoE. Best case, OptiQ nudges 27B from
   38.3% toward its bf16 ceiling; even Qwen's own (n>1, thinking-mode) numbers put
   dense 27B only ~3.5 pts above MoE, and section 4 shows that ordering inverts in
   single-shot non-thinking deployment. The realistic headroom is sub-2-point, and
   the workload that matters escalates to cloud at 84% anyway.

There is no routing tier that wants this model: C2 is the 35B-A3B MoE; anything
needing more quality goes to cloud. A 27B dense slot between them is exactly the slot
the matrix already deleted.

## Phase-1 measurement + pre-registered kill rule (test-null-hypothesis-first)

Per platform doctrine, even a "likely-moot" spike gets a falsifiable hypothesis and a
kill condition before any multi-hour A/B. The whole point: do **not** spend the ~4h
27B matrix re-run unless a cheap upstream check survives.

**Hypothesis (the testable claim that would justify the work):**
> Qwen3.6-27B-OptiQ-4bit lands a *deployable* config — i.e., it beats the current
> best local (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-4bit, 40.0% pass@1 @ 15.4s median) on quality with
> decode speed within 1.5× of the MoE, on the LCB v6 deployment-realistic config.

**Phase-1 (cheap, ~30–60 min, no full matrix):**
1. **Existence + sizing gate (5 min).** Confirm `mlx-community/Qwen3.6-27B-OptiQ-4bit`
   exists, has non-trivial downloads / a community signal, and read its
   `optiq_metadata.json` (per-layer bit allocation) — same artifact already present
   for the 0.8B variant. If it still has zero adoption or was never published, stop.
2. **Decode-speed probe (20–30 min).** Load it once, run a single ~2K-token coding
   prompt at `max_tokens=8192`, measure median tok/s and gen-time. Compare against the
   recorded 27B plain (62.3s) and 35B-A3B (15.4s) from the matrix. No correctness run.
3. **Headroom arithmetic.** Plain 27B is 38.3%. OptiQ's ceiling is the 27B bf16 score.
   Estimate it from OptiQ's published 4bit→OptiQ recovery on comparable Qwen models
   (the +X claim in the card). If 38.3% + plausible-recovery < 40.0%, the quality case
   is dead on paper.

**KILL RULE (pre-registered — close ep8 MOOT, file no followups, if ANY holds):**
- (a) The OptiQ-27B card does not exist / has zero adoption / was withdrawn; **or**
- (b) Phase-1 decode probe shows >1.5× slower than 35B-A3B (it will — dense vs MoE);
  **or**
- (c) Phase-1 arithmetic shows OptiQ's plausible quality ceiling for 27B ≤ 40.0%
  (35B-A3B's score), i.e. no path to beating the incumbent local.

Meeting the kill rule is the *expected* outcome. Only if all three fail — OptiQ-27B
exists with adoption, decodes within 1.5× of the MoE, AND has a credible path above
40.0% — does the full ~4h LCB v6 A/B (step 3 of the bead) become worth running.
