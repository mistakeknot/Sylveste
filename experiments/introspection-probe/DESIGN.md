# Introspection Probe — Flagship Design

> Is a model's **stated confidence grounded in an internal uncertainty
> representation, or confabulated after the fact?**
>
> Status: **design sketch.** Needs interp tooling (nnsight/transformer-lens) + a GPU +
> behavioral runs from `../calibration-eval` to seed it. Nothing here runs yet.

---

## 1. Context & question

The calibration eval (`../calibration-eval`) measures *whether* a model's verbalized
confidence tracks its own correctness (type-2 sensitivity, meta-d′). It treats the
model as a black box: it scores the **report**. It cannot tell us *where the report
comes from*.

The flagship asks the next, deeper question — the one that is actually about
**metacognition as a mechanism**, not a behavior:

> When a model says "CONFIDENCE: 80", is it **reading out an internal signal that
> represents its own uncertainty**, or is the number generated by surface heuristics
> (question phrasing, hedge words, answer length) with no causal link to an internal
> "feeling of knowing"?

This is the introspection question in Anthropic's sense (does self-report correspond to
internal state) and the metacognition question in the systems-neuro sense (is there a
second-order readout of first-order processing). A clean result either way is
publishable and distinctive:

- **Grounded:** the report reads from a decodable uncertainty representation → models
  have a form of genuine introspective access to their own reliability.
- **Confabulated:** the report is decoupled from the internal signal that actually
  predicts correctness → stated confidence is post-hoc narration, and a *better* signal
  exists inside that we're not eliciting.

## 2. Why now / why this stack

- **Open weights unlock it.** This is the payoff of the Nous/Hermes pivot: behavioral
  calibration runs on any API, but reading and *intervening on* internal activations
  needs open weights. Hermes models on HF are the substrate.
- **The eval is the target-finder.** The flagship does not start from scratch — it
  reuses the calibration eval's items, correctness labels, and metrics, and focuses its
  most expensive analysis on the **dissociation items** the eval surfaces (where
  internal and verbalized signals disagree). The eval earns its keep as Step 1.

## 3. Conceptual frame: the grounding chain

"Grounded introspection" is not one claim but a causal chain. We test each link:

| Link | Claim | Method | Falsifiable form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Existence** | An internal signal encodes the model's *own* correctness | linear probe on activations | probe type-2 AUROC > 0.5 beyond difficulty controls |
| **Headroom** | That signal knows *more than the model says* | compare probe vs verbalized AUROC/meta-d′ | AUROC_probe > AUROC_verbalized |
| **Coupling** | The report **reads from** the signal (not merely parallel to it) | activation steering / patching | steering the signal shifts stated confidence |

The third link is the crux. Correlation between an internal signal and the report is
*not* introspection — they could be two parallel readouts of the same upstream cause.
Only the **intervention** distinguishes "the report is generated by reading the signal"
from "the report happens to agree with the signal."

## 4. The three probes

### Probe A — Decode an internal confidence signal (representational)

- Run the model over the eval items; capture residual-stream activations at the
  **answer-commitment token** (the token that fixes the multiple-choice letter), swept
  across layers.
- Train a **linear probe** to predict per-item correctness (0/1) from those activations.
  The probe's predicted P(correct) is the model's *internal confidence*.
- Neuro transfer (the distinctive lens): characterize the **geometry** — is uncertainty
  a single linear "confidence axis" or distributed/curved? Use representational-geometry
  / RSA and dimensionality of the correctness-predictive subspace. (Direction 1 of the
  brief, applied in service of the metacognition question.)
- Layer sweep → where does own-correctness become linearly decodable, and does it peak
  *before* the verbalization tokens are produced (temporal priority for "headroom")?

### Probe B — Dissociation (does internal beat / lead verbalized?)

- Put three correctness-discriminators on the **same type-2 footing** (reuse
  `calibration-eval/src/metrics.py`): verbalized confidence, token-logprob confidence,
  and the Probe-A internal signal.
- If `meta-d′(internal) > meta-d′(verbalized)`, the model "knows more than it says" —
  introspective headroom; the report is lossy.
- Select the **dissociation set**: items where internal says *uncertain* but verbalized
  says *confident* (and vice versa). These are the confabulation-candidate items and the
  focus of Probe C. This is the concrete bridge from Step 1's per-item logs.

### Probe C — Causal coupling (steering / patching)

- Find the "confidence direction" from Probe A (e.g. probe weight vector, or
  diff-of-means between high/low internal-confidence activations).
- **Intervene:** add ±α·direction at the relevant layer(s), then re-elicit the
  verbalized confidence (and re-read the answer).
- **Grounded** if stated confidence moves monotonically with α (a "confidence dial");
  **confabulated** if the report is unmoved while the decodable signal is clearly
  shifted.
- Controls: random-direction steering (should do nothing), norm-matching, and a
  capability check (steering shouldn't just break the model). Cross-check whether the
  *answer* changes too — tells us if we hit a shared correctness signal or a report-only
  knob.

## 5. Hypotheses (pre-register before running)

- **H1 (existence):** linear probe decodes own-correctness above chance in mid-late
  layers, beyond input-difficulty controls.
- **H2 (headroom):** `meta-d′(internal) > meta-d′(verbalized)` — models internally
  discriminate correct/incorrect better than they verbalize.
- **H3 (grounding):** steering the probe direction shifts verbalized confidence
  (coupling) — **or it does not (confabulation).** *The design makes a null informative*:
  decoupling is itself the finding that stated confidence is post-hoc.
- **H4 (domain structure — ties to RQ2):** grounding/headroom differ by `domain_type` —
  e.g. a crisp internal signal on `verifiable_technical`, weaker/decoupled on
  `consensus_recall` (no internal "truth" to read). This re-connects the flagship to the
  eval's domain story.

## 7. Tooling & compute (the real gate)

- **Interp:** `nnsight` (multi-model, lighter) or `transformer-lens`; `nnterp` wrapper.
- **Models:** Hermes open weights on HF (confirm the exact checkpoint exists on the
  NousResearch HF org — portal API access ≠ weight access).
- **Compute: the user's RTX 4090 (24 GB), Windows desktop via WSL2.** No rental needed.
  This workload is forward passes + activation capture + linear-probe fits + steering —
  no training, so no optimizer/gradient memory. Envelope:
  - **~7–8B Hermes in bf16** (≈14–16 GB weights + KV/activations) — the workhorse;
    full precision, which is what clean interp wants. Target this tier.
  - **~13–14B only via 8-bit** (~14 GB) — acceptable for the *decode* probes (A/B);
    **avoid for steering (C)**: quantization perturbs the very activations being
    manipulated.
  - **70B/405B out of scope** for activation work — the *mechanistic* ladder is capped
    at the small tier; note this asymmetry vs the behavioral ladder.
  - Activation storage is not a bottleneck: capture at a single locus token and
    offload to CPU/disk.
- **Environment:** run the stack inside **WSL2** (Ubuntu + NVIDIA CUDA-on-WSL driver);
  the interp ecosystem (nnsight/transformer-lens/bitsandbytes wheels) is Linux-first
  and native Windows hits dependency walls. `tmux` for long jobs; disable Windows sleep.
- **Remote driving:** cloud sessions can reach the desktop over Tailscale —
  feasibility verified from a session container (root + CAP_NET_ADMIN + /dev/net/tun +
  egress to the Tailscale control plane/DERP/pkgs all confirmed). Wiring:
  `scripts/join-tailnet.sh` joins the tailnet using an **ephemeral, tagged,
  ACL-scoped `TS_AUTHKEY`** env secret, then drives WSL2 via Tailscale SSH
  (`tailscale up --ssh` on the desktop side — one-time user setup). Deferred until
  Step 2 starts, by decision.

## 8. Confounds & controls (preempt the reviewer)

- **Difficulty leakage:** the probe might read *input difficulty*, not the model's *own*
  uncertainty. Control by partialling out item difficulty, and by using items where
  samples/models disagree (own-uncertainty varies with input held fixed).
- **Locus choice:** which token / layer to read and steer — sweep, pre-register the
  selection rule, avoid double-dipping (fit probe and evaluate on held-out items).
- **Steering validity:** norm-matched, random-direction control, capability sanity check.
- **Temporal ordering:** confidence is verbalized *after* the answer in our prompt, so
  the answer-token internal signal genuinely precedes the report — supports "leads."

## 9. Milestones

1. Stand up nnsight on a small Hermes; capture activations for the custom set; confirm

2. Probe A: per-layer correctness decoding + geometry; pick the locus. *(H1)*
3. Probe B: internal vs verbalized vs logprob meta-d′; export dissociation set. *(H2)*
4. Probe C: steering harness + dose-response on stated confidence; controls. *(H3)*
